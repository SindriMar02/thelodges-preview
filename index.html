( Akureyri, Laugar )

The Lodges are small places to stay in North Iceland, run by Stella & Eyþór, the people behind Villa North. The first is a timber house from 1896 in the old town of Akureyri. The second is on its way at Laugar, in the valley of Reykjadalur.

akureyrilodge.isExplore Akureyri Lodge

Akureyri Lodge

A green timber house in Innbærinn, the oldest part of Akureyri, rebuilt by hand and among the best kept houses in town. Three apartments, each with its own kitchen and bathroom, a garden with a hot tub, and the town, the harbour and the botanical garden on foot. The art collection stays with the house.
