The story

Since 1896

Built by a merchant and his wife, rebuilt by hand a century later and now among the best kept houses in town.

1821

The Lilliendahl house stands on the plot.

1896

Sigfús Jónsson and Marselína Kristjánsdóttir build the present house.

1898

Anna Magnúsdóttir opens a photo studio in the shed behind it.

1923

A vet downstairs, a baker upstairs: Hlíðarshúsið.

1942

Twenty-two people live under its roof.

2001

Rebuilt by hand, on new foundations.

2003

The town’s preservation award.

2012

The highest preservation value in the building survey.
